The core of my graduate research at the University of Science and Technology of China investigates the intersection of **blockchain security** and **regulatory technology (RegTech)** — asking how distributed ledger systems can be hardened against adversarial threats while being leveraged to automate and strengthen compliance in financial systems.

Key threads of this research include smart contract vulnerability analysis, consensus-layer attack surfaces, and how on-chain transparency can be paired with cryptographic privacy techniques to satisfy regulatory reporting requirements without exposing sensitive data.
